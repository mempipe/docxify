Type to edit. Enter, Backspace and Delete change paragraphs. ⌘B, ⌘I and ⌘U format a selection. ⌘⌥1 to ⌘⌥3 give headings, ⌘⌥0 the default style. ⌘⇧8 and ⌘⇧7 make bullets and numbering, and Tab and Shift+Tab change a list item's level. ⌘K links a selection, and ⌘-click opens a link. ⌘⌥M comments on a selection or the word at the caret, and Reply in a comment's balloon answers it. Clicking a form field fills it in, and a check box ticks or clears. Picture, or pasting an image, puts a picture at the caret. In a table, the table buttons add and delete rows and columns. Track changes records typing and deleting under your name. Update fields on open asks Word to update fields, such as a table of contents, when it opens the downloaded file. ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z undoes.
